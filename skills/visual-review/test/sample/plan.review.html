Add login to the app

Source: test/sample/plan.md · 4 phases · ~9 days

TL;DR

  • Email + password sign-in so saved trades sync across devices; guests keep working.
  • 4 phases, about 9 days: backend first, then screens, then guest-data migration.
  • Biggest risk: duplicated trades when migrating guest data.
  • Two open questions: Google sign-in, and how long "remember me" lasts.

Phases

  1. Accounts 2 days
    Users table, argon2 password hashing, sign-up endpoint.
  2. Sessions 2 days
    Login endpoint, HttpOnly session cookies, logout.
  3. Screens 3 days
    Login and sign-up screens, "remember me".
  4. Guest data 2 days
    Move guest data into the new account on first login.

Login flow

Email + password
Server checks hash
HttpOnly session cookie
Load saved trades

Risks

Guest data migration high

Logging in on two devices at once could duplicate trades. Migration must be idempotent.

No password reset medium

Out of scope until phase 5 — users who forget their password are stuck.

Password guessing planned

Rate limit /login to 5 attempts per minute per IP.

Open questions

Google sign-in in the first version?

"Remember me" lifetime: 30 or 90 days?
